Frequently asked questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade at any time, and cancel a paid plan from your account page — you'll keep access until the end of your current billing period.

What happens if I hit my daily limit?

You'll see a clear notice when a limit is reached, and it resets automatically at midnight UTC. Upgrading raises your limits immediately.

What payment methods are supported?

Payments are processed securely through Paystack, supporting cards, bank transfers, and other methods available in your region.

Is my data used to train models?

No. Your conversations are used only to generate your responses and are not used for model training.
