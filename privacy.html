Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Cognita AI ("Cognita", "we", "us", or "our") provides an AI-powered assistant and related services. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create or use a Cognita account, we may collect information necessary to provide and secure the service, such as your name, email address, authentication information, and account identifiers.

Conversation and User Content

We process information that you voluntarily provide to Cognita, including prompts, messages, uploaded content, generated content, and other information necessary to operate features that you request.

Depending on the features you use, conversations and generated content may be stored so that you can access your conversations and previously generated results.

Technical Information

We may collect technical information necessary to operate, maintain, secure, and improve the service, such as browser information, device information, IP address, timestamps, authentication events, error information, and service usage information.

2. How We Use Information

We use information we process to:

  • provide and operate Cognita;
  • authenticate users and protect accounts;
  • process prompts and generate requested AI responses;
  • provide features requested by the user;
  • store and retrieve conversations and generated content where applicable;
  • integrate with services that the user explicitly connects;
  • detect, investigate, and prevent abuse, fraud, and security incidents;
  • maintain and improve the reliability and performance of the service; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Google Account Integrations

Cognita may allow you to connect supported Google services through Google's OAuth authorization system. Connecting a Google account is optional and requires your authorization.

When you authorize a Google integration, Cognita requests only the Google permissions necessary for the particular functionality it provides.

Google Calendar

If you connect Google Calendar, Cognita may access calendar and event information necessary to provide Calendar features that you request. This can include calendar names and identifiers, event titles, descriptions, dates and times, locations, attendees, recurrence information, and other event information needed to display, search, create, update, or manage calendar events.

Depending on the authorization granted and the feature being used, Cognita may read calendar information and may create, update, or delete calendar events at your request.

Gmail

If you connect Gmail, Cognita may use Gmail functionality necessary for features you explicitly request, such as sending email, managing Gmail labels, and performing other supported Gmail actions available under the permissions you authorize.

Cognita does not request permission to generally read the contents of your Gmail mailbox as part of the Gmail integration described by this policy. Cognita's Gmail functionality is limited to the permissions and capabilities actually granted through Google's OAuth authorization.

Google User Data

Google data accessed through Cognita is used only to provide the Google-related functionality that you request, to maintain and secure that functionality, and for purposes permitted by Google's applicable policies.

Cognita does not sell Google user data. We do not use Google user data for advertising or to determine a person's eligibility for credit, insurance, employment, housing, or similar decisions.

Cognita does not use Google user data to train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

Google user data is not transferred to third parties except where necessary to provide a requested feature, to maintain and secure the service, as directed by you, or where otherwise permitted or required by applicable law.

Cognita's handling of information obtained through Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy , including its Limited Use requirements.

4. AI Processing

Cognita uses artificial-intelligence services to process prompts and generate responses. Information necessary to fulfill a request may be transmitted to the AI infrastructure or service provider used to perform that request.

Information obtained from a connected service is processed only when necessary to provide the feature you request. For example, if you ask Cognita to perform an authorized Calendar action, the information necessary to understand and execute that request may be processed by the systems used to provide Cognita's AI functionality.

Google user data is not used to train generalized AI models.

5. Storage and Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested services, maintain account functionality, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and protect the security of Cognita.

Conversation content and generated files may remain stored according to the retention and deletion functionality provided by Cognita.

When you disconnect a connected Google account or revoke authorization, Cognita will no longer be able to use the revoked Google authorization to access the corresponding Google service. Information that was previously processed or stored may remain where necessary for legitimate operational, security, legal, or user-requested purposes, subject to applicable retention and deletion procedures.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

OAuth credentials and access tokens are handled using server-side application mechanisms designed to prevent unnecessary exposure to client-side code.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Sharing of Information

We may share information with service providers that help us operate Cognita, such as infrastructure, hosting, authentication, storage, security, analytics, and AI service providers, when such sharing is necessary to provide or secure the service.

We do not sell your personal information.

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal process, protect the rights or safety of users or others, investigate abuse or security incidents, or protect Cognita and its infrastructure.

8. Your Choices

You may choose whether to connect supported third-party services to Cognita. You can also revoke Cognita's access through the relevant third-party account settings.

You may use Cognita without connecting optional third-party services, although features that depend on those services will not be available.

9. Third-Party Services

Cognita may integrate with third-party services to provide functionality requested by users. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Examples include authentication providers, cloud infrastructure providers, AI service providers, Google services, and other services required to operate particular Cognita features.

10. Children's Privacy

Cognita is not intended for children under the age at which they can lawfully use the service without parental consent under applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable legal requirements.

11. International Processing

Depending on the infrastructure and service providers used to operate Cognita, information may be processed or stored in countries other than the country in which you reside.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Cognita, applicable law, or our privacy practices. When material changes are made, we may provide notice through the service or by other appropriate means.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Cognita's privacy practices, please contact us through the contact method provided on the Cognita website.
