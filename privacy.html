1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how Cognita ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and application (the "Service"). It applies to all users of Cognita.

2. Information We Collect

Account information. When you sign up, we collect your email address and, if you sign in with Google, basic profile information (name, email, profile photo) provided by Google's authentication service. We do not receive or store your Google password.

Usage data. We record which features you use and how often, including the number of messages, images, and documents generated per day, in order to enforce plan limits and improve the Service. We do not store the full content of your conversations beyond what is necessary to generate a response, unless you have explicitly saved that content within the Service.

Payment information. Payments are processed by Paystack. We do not collect or store your full card details — Paystack handles this directly, and we only receive confirmation of successful or failed transactions, a customer reference code, and the plan you subscribed to.

Content you provide. This includes text prompts, uploaded files, and any other input you submit to generate a response ("Input"), and the responses generated in return ("Output").

Technical data. We may collect standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, and device information for security, fraud prevention, and diagnostic purposes.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information above to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Service;
  • Authenticate your account and keep it secure;
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions;
  • Enforce plan entitlements and usage limits;
  • Respond to support requests;
  • Improve the reliability, performance, and safety of the Service;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your Input or Output to train AI models, ours or any third party's.

4. How Your Requests Are Processed

When you send a message or request to Cognita, it is processed by our backend infrastructure, which may route the request to one or more third-party AI infrastructure providers to generate a response. These providers process your Input solely to generate a response and, per their own data processing terms, are not permitted to use it to train their models on our behalf. We select and may change these providers at our discretion to maintain service quality and reliability.

5. Data Sharing

We share information only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the Service, including our authentication provider, database provider, AI infrastructure providers, and payment processor (Paystack), each of whom is contractually or by policy restricted to using your data only to provide their service to us;
  • Legal authorities, where required to comply with applicable law, legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Cognita, our users, or others;
  • A successor entity, in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy continuing to govern your information.

We do not share your information with advertisers, and Cognita does not display third-party advertising.

6. Data Retention

We retain account information for as long as your account is active. Usage counters are retained only as long as needed to enforce daily limits and are not kept indefinitely. Payment records are retained as required for accounting, tax, and fraud-prevention purposes. You may request deletion of your account and associated data as described in Section 8.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) for all traffic, server-side verification of every authenticated request, and restricted access to production systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, and request a copy of your data in a portable format. To exercise these rights, contact us at YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe and in accordance with applicable law.

You can delete your account at any time by contacting support; this will remove your account record and stop any further billing, subject to retention required for legal or accounting purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

10. International Data Transfers

Our infrastructure providers may process data in countries other than your own. Where this occurs, we rely on those providers' own compliance measures and, where applicable, standard contractual safeguards to protect your information consistent with this Policy.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated by posting a notice on the Service or updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the Service after such changes constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.

12. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled can be sent to YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL.
