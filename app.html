Remembered voices

This is off unless you choose it. After you name a speaker on a finished note, you can tap "Remember this voice".     Delete your account?

    This permanently deletes your Cognita account and everything in it. There is no way to undo this or recover your data afterward.

    Deleting your account removes:

    • Your chat history and saved conversations
    • Resources, flashcards, and documents you created
    • Reminders and notification subscriptions
    • Scheduled social posts and AI Inbox items
    • Any connected accounts (Google, GitHub, Facebook/Instagram, Canva) and everything Cognita stored from them
    • Your subscription and billing record
    • Your sign-in itself — you'll need to create a new account to use Cognita again

    If you have an active paid subscription, you'll need to cancel it from the Plan section above first — Cognita won't delete an account that's still being billed.
