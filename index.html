The product

One place for the work around your thinking.

The idea is rarely the hard part. The work is reading the source, checking the numbers, learning what you don't know, writing it up and following through. Cognita keeps all of it in one workspace.

Think

Work the problem, not just the prompt.

Ask a question, then keep going. Use Standard for quick answers, or Advanced and Thorough when a decision needs real reasoning. Cognita sets out the options, weighs the trade-offs, and questions the assumption that doesn't hold up.

Pricing decisionAdvanced

Customers are leaving after month two. Do we discount, wait, or contact the at-risk accounts?

Offer a blanket discountQuick to do, but it costs margin on people who would have stayed.
Wait and seeCheapest, but the rising ticket volume suggests this isn't noise.
Contact at-risk accounts directlyTargets the accounts whose usage is falling. The cost is mostly time.

One assumption to check first: are the accounts with falling usage the same ones filing tickets?

What happens to your work.

It isn't used for training.
We don't use your prompts, uploads or generated content to train AI models, and we don't sell your personal information.
Access is checked on our servers.
Every request is authenticated server-side, so who can see what is never decided by your browser.
Code stays on your device.
When Cognita runs Python or JavaScript for you, it runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded unless you download it or ask Cognita to read the result.
Payments go through Paystack.
We don't see or store your full card details.
Disconnecting means disconnecting.
When you disconnect an app, the access tokens we held for it are deleted.
You can leave.
Cancel from your account whenever you like, and delete your account from Settings.
