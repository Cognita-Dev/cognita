Think

Work the problem, not just the prompt.

Ask a question, then keep going. Use Standard for quick answers, or Advanced and Thorough when a decision needs real reasoning. Cognita sets out the options, weighs the trade-offs, and questions the assumption that doesn't hold up.

Pricing decisionAdvanced

Customers are leaving after month two. Do we discount, wait, or contact the at-risk accounts?

Offer a blanket discountQuick to do, but it costs margin on people who would have stayed.
Wait and seeCheapest, but the rising ticket volume suggests this isn't noise.
Contact at-risk accounts directlyTargets the accounts whose usage is falling. The cost is mostly time.

One assumption to check first: are the accounts with falling usage the same ones filing tickets?

Q3 Retention Brief

Prepared for the leadership team · Q3 2026

Summary

Churn rose from 3.1% in July to 4.6% in September. The causes are concentrated in three places: onboarding, billing and a pricing page that hides the annual plan.

What we found

  • New accounts that skip the data import are the most likely to cancel.
  • Setup and billing questions make up most support tickets.
  • Annual plans are rarely chosen because the toggle is below the fold.

Recommendation

Contact at-risk accounts directly before offering any discount, and ship a guided import this week.
