What you can do

More useful than a chat box.

Cognita is built for the work that usually happens before, between, and after the meetings.

Make the document

Need a report, proposal, memo, letter, or brief? Ask for it and get a proper Word document you can open, edit, and send.

Show, don't just explain

Turn complicated ideas and processes into diagrams and visuals when a picture will make the point faster.

Dig into a question

Compare options, examine the details, find the gaps in an argument, and work through a problem before making a call.

Write for the person reading it

A note to your team shouldn't sound like a thesis. A research paper shouldn't sound like an advert. Cognita adjusts to what you're writing and who it's for.

Push back when it matters

You don't need another yes-man. Cognita can question an assumption, flag a weak point, or show you another way of looking at the problem.

Your work stays yours

Your conversations aren't used to train AI models. Requests are authenticated and processed securely on the server.
